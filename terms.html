Terms and Conditions

TrendingTVBackdrops is a small hobby app that posts a weekly set of trending movie and TV backdrop images to the subreddit r/WeeklyTVBackdrops. It is run by the moderators of that subreddit.

Use

The app is provided as is, with no guarantees of availability or accuracy. It posts images only to the subreddit it is installed on and deletes its own posts from the previous week.

Content

Images and film and TV information come from TMDB. This product uses TMDB and the TMDB APIs but is not endorsed, certified, or otherwise approved by TMDB. All artwork belongs to its respective owners.

Removal requests

If you own rights to any image and want it removed, contact us using the details below and it will be taken down promptly.
